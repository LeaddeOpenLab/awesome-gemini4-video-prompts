## Prompt (source language)

```text
Build a single self-contained HTML file: a simple 3D "Jelly Pumpkin" demo using three.js from a CDN. Keep the code short and basic.  SCENE - One orange pumpkin in the centre made from a low-poly sphere (about 24x16 segments) with 8 shallow ribs, flat shading. - MeshPhongMaterial, slightly transparent (opacity 0.9), bright orange with a little emissive colour. No transmission, no environment map, no post-processing. - Jack-o'-lantern face made of flat black 2D shapes (two triangle eyes, triangle nose, zigzag mouth) placed just in front of the pumpkin surface. No glow from inside. - Simple green cylinder stem on top. - Three small low-poly pumpkins (purple, green, orange) and four small flattened spheres as candy around it. - White background, ambient light plus one directional light, basic hard shadows on the floor.  INTERACTION - Click the pumpkin: it squashes slightly (scale Y down, X/Z up) and springs back quickly with no extra wobble. - Drag the stem: draw a straight thin green cylinder from the stem to the cursor. Dragging upward lifts the whole pumpkin rigidly; release and it falls, with one small bounce. - "Carve Face" button: instantly swap to a second face (two black circle eyes, round mouth). - "Glow" slider: changes the emissive intensity of the pumpkin a little. - "Add Candy" button: drops 16 small spheres from above; they fall, bounce once on the floor and stop. No collision with the pumpkin.  UI - Plain HTML: title "Jelly Pumpkin" in bold Arial top left, subtitle "Click the pumpkin to make it wobble!". - Top right white box with border: colour buttons (Orange, Purple, Green), Glow range input, "Carve Face" and "Add Candy" buttons with default browser styling. - Bottom left small grey text with stats: "Wobble: 0.6 | Face: 1 | Candy: 0".
```
