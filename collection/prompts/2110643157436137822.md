## Prompt (source language)

```text
Build a playable recreation of the final wand duel between Harry Potter and Lord Voldemort (the beam clash at the end of Deathly Hallows Part 2) in a single HTML file. three.js from a CDN is fine; everything else must be generated in code: no images, no audio files, no web fonts, no film music or logos.

The scene:
- The ruined Hogwarts courtyard at dawn after the battle: broken stone arches and pillars, rubble, scorched ground, drifting embers, dust and smoke, cold blue dawn light with a warm glow from the duel. A low cinematic side camera with both wizards in frame.
- Harry on the left: a young wizard in a dark torn jacket, round glasses, wand arm extended. Voldemort on the right: tall, pale, bald, long flowing black robes. Build both from simple 3D shapes as stylized, instantly recognizable silhouettes; robes and jacket move in the wind.
- Harry casts a red spell (Expelliarmus), Voldemort a green one (Avada Kedavra). The two beams meet in a blazing knot of light that crackles, pulses and throws sparks; thin lightning arcs, a shockwave ring every few seconds, camera shake, a bloom-like glow (additive blending).

The game:
- No menu: the duel starts immediately with the beams already locked, so the very first frame is full of light and motion.
- The player is Harry: mash SPACE (or click/tap) to push the clash point toward Voldemort. Voldemort pushes back with rising strength and sudden surges, so the knot slides back and forth.
- Show the struggle: the knot grows brighter and bigger as it moves; the losing side's beam flickers and thins, the winning side's beam thickens. A slim power bar at the bottom and a short hint "MASH SPACE".
- When the knot reaches Voldemort, his curse rebounds: a slow-motion moment, a huge red flash, his wand spins out of his hand and he breaks apart into black ash flakes that drift away in the wind. Big title: "EXPELLIARMUS". If the knot reaches Harry instead: a green flash and "AVADA KEDAVRA". Press R to duel again.
- Sound synthesized live with the Web Audio API (no samples): a rising roar of the two beams whose pitch follows the struggle, crackles, shockwave thumps and the final boom; start audio on the first key press or click if the browser blocks autoplay.
- Demo mode: if the URL contains ?demo, Harry is played by a scripted player that mashes like a human, so the whole duel plays out in about 25 seconds, swings back and forth at least twice and ends with Harry winning.
- Runs smoothly at 60 fps in a laptop browser and fills the window (1920x1080 and other sizes).
```
