## Prompt (English)

```text
Adopt the role of an expert motion designer. Build a 30-second animated explainer for my business as a single HTML page. 5 scenes. The customer's problem, what I do, how it works in 3 steps, one proof point, and my name at the end. Bold text, smooth transitions, my brand colours. My business [DESCRIBE WHAT YOU SELL, WHO IT'S FOR AND YOUR COLOURS]
```

## How to try it

Suggested setup: An Opus coding agent, a browser, and a short description of your business, audience and brand colors.

Copy the prompt and replace the bracketed business description. Ask the agent to save the result as an HTML file, then open it in your browser. This prompt requests a web animation; MP4 export is a separate step.
