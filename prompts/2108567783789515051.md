## Prompt (English)

```text
You are a world-class cinematic Motion Design Director, After Effects Art Director, 3D Scientific Visualization Director, and VFX Director.

I want to create a high-end scientific music animation about the Nobel Prize in Physics based on the SRT lyrics I provide.

SRT is the only basis for timing.

Do not assume, modify, or generate timelines on your own.
Do not output fixed timecodes.
Do not mechanically map one lyric line to one shot.

You must autonomously design shot durations, editing rhythm, camera changes, and visual climaxes based on actual lyric onset times, pauses, repetitions, rhythm, and musical structure in the SRT.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[1. Final Visual Goal]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

What I want is not an ordinary popular science animation.
Not a PPT.
Not an infographic.
Not children's science.
Not a simple 3D model display.
Not an AI tech template.

What I want is:
Cinematic AE Motion Design
+ Top-tier 3D VFX
+ Apple TV+ science documentary texture
+ High-end tech keynote visual aesthetic
+ IMAX science documentary scale
+ Modern music MV rhythm
+ Houdini / Cinema 4D / After Effects level visual effects

Overall feeling:
Stunning, Mysterious, Premium, Modern, Grand, Precise, Cinematic, Futuristic, Scientific.

The visual should make the audience think:
"This is not an ordinary YouTube explainer, this is a cinematic science visual blockbuster."

But the spectacle must be grounded in scientific logic.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[2. Core Narrative]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The entire animation must revolve around a complete visual story:

Distant universe generates neutrinos
↓
Neutrinos barely interact with ordinary matter
↓
They can penetrate massive celestial bodies and even Earth
↓
This also makes them extremely difficult to detect
↓
Scientists bury massive numbers of optical detectors deep in Antarctic ice
↓
Forming the IceCube Neutrino Observatory
↓
A rare neutrino interacts with an atom in the ice
↓
Producing a charged particle
↓
The charged particle produces Cherenkov light in ice
↓
Detectors record photon arrival positions and times
↓
Massive data forms a spatial trajectory
↓
Tracing the neutrino's incoming direction from trajectory and time data
↓
Ultimately tracing back to high-energy celestial objects in the distant universe
↓
Humanity gains a new way to observe the universe

The whole video must let general audiences understand this logic. Not just look scientific, but be truly comprehensible.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[3. Most Important Visual Concept]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Establish a single core visual DNA across the entire piece:
"The blue neutrino trajectory."

Do not design the neutrino as an ordinary glowing ball.
It should be: ultra-thin, high-speed, mysterious, almost invisible, with a subtle blue-white glow, like a ghost trajectory from the depths of the universe.

This trajectory runs through the entire video:
passing through nebulae, supernovae, near black holes, through Earth, through human bodies, entering Antarctica, traversing 2 km of ice, and leaving an observable blue trace in IceCube.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[4. Visual Style]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Primary colors: Pure black, Deep space blue, Deep gray, Ice blue, Silver white.
Sole primary accent color: Electric Blue / Cherenkov Blue.

Prohibited: Cheap rainbow gradients, cyberpunk purple/pink, excessive RGB, overt neon, cheap glow, game UI, tech templates, heavy HUDs, meaningless complex numbers, PPT charts.

Must have abundant black negative space to keep light, particles, and ice as visual focal points.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[5. Cinematic Cinematography]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Avoid static shots. Use cinematic visual language: Extreme Wide Shot, Macro Shot, Extreme Macro, Deep Space Fly Through, Orbital Camera, Long Lens, Dolly In/Out, Tracking Shot, Crash Zoom, Whip Pan, Parallax, Volumetric Camera, Micro-to-Macro, Continuous Camera Movement.

Emphasize scale transitions across: Universe → Galaxy → Star → Earth → Antarctica → Ice Sheet → Detector → Atom → Particle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[6. Cosmic Section]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Space scenes must serve the concept that neutrinos originate from extreme environments: supernovae, black hole accretion disks, relativistic jets, AGNs, gravitational lensing, spacetime curvature.

Extract a fine blue neutrino trajectory from an immense cosmic event and follow it with the camera.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[7. Neutrino Penetrating Earth]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Camera rapidly approaches Earth from space. Earth turns semi-transparent, revealing crust, mantle, outer core, inner core. Neutrino passes straight through with volumetric light and realistic layering, exiting the opposite side.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[8. Why Difficult to Detect]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Contrast visually: photons hit/reflect/absorb; other particles interact; neutrinos pass right through. Repeat, leaving darkness and a faint blue point.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[9. Antarctic Ice Sheet]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cinematic ice dive from space down into Antarctica's ice depth, showing real refraction, scattering, crystal structure, cracks, air bubbles, and volumetric light, plunging into total darkness before IceCube detectors light up one by one.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[10. IceCube as a Visual Icon]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Design as an architectural, temple-like, futuristic 3D detector grid spanning kilometers with strong scale contrasts.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[11. The Most Important Scientific VFX]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Neutrino enters ice; camera dives to extreme macro (ice → molecule → atom → nucleus). Neutrino interacts → charged particle created → travels faster than light in ice → Cherenkov blue light cone forms, illuminating surrounding ice crystals and triggering IceCube sensors.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[12. Blue Light and Detectors]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Detectors light up sequentially according to physical arrival times, producing faint blue halos as camera flies through.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[13. Abstracting Principles into Visuals]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Show Cherenkov light detection, recording 3D coordinates and arrival timestamps, organically synthesizing the reconstructed particle trajectory.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[14. Timing-Difference Localization]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Visualize wavefronts, timestamps, and 3D paths merging into a directional arrow extending back through ice, Earth, and space pointing to the distant cosmic origin (SOURCE RECONSTRUCTION).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[15. Final Cosmic Scale]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Camera pulls back from IceCube → Antarctica → Earth → Moon orbit → Solar System → Milky Way → deep cosmos, connecting the tiny blue point to the vast universe.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[16. Final Visual Climax]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Black void with blue neutrino trajectory branching into LIGHT, GRAVITATIONAL WAVES, NEUTRINOS. First two fade, highlighting NEUTRINOS, concluding with "A NEW WINDOW TO THE UNIVERSE".

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[17. Motion Design]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Utilize particle systems, point clouds, volumetric light, depth of field, motion blur, procedural animation, physics-based motion with inertia and easing.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[18. SRT-Driven Timing]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

I will provide an SRT file as the sole time reference. Automatically determine shot durations, transitions, and pacing based on lyrics.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[19. Visual Rhythm]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Verse: mysterious, restrained.
Pre-chorus: accelerating particles and camera motion.
Chorus: grand, explosive blue light, massive detector grid.
Science section: precise macro clarity.
Final chorus: cosmic scale release.
Outro: absolute silence, black void, single vanishing blue trajectory.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[20. Typography]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Cinematic modern sans-serif title design, emphasizing keywords (NEUTRINO, ICECUBE, 2 KM, CHERENKOV LIGHT, TIME, POSITION, DIRECTION, SOURCE) in white and ice blue with ample negative space.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[21. Scientific Accuracy]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve scientific rigor: do not claim neutrinos are massless; do not depict neutrinos as ordinary photons; Cherenkov radiation comes from secondary charged particles traveling through ice.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
[22. Aesthetic Standards & Final Deliverables]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Output:
1. Full visual narrative structure
2. Visual concept per lyric section
3. Shot-by-shot visual breakdown
4. Camera movement
5. 3D Scene setup
6. VFX and particle design
7. Lighting
8. Transitions
9. Typography
10. Motion design
11. Implementation advice for AE, Blender/C4D/Houdini
12. Visual climaxes and breathing pauses
13. Unifying visual DNA.
```
