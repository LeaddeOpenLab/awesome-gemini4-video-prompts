## Prompt (source language)

```text
Build a complete playable 3D miniature city destruction sandbox as a web app. Use Three.js and realistic-feeling arcade physics.

Create a beautifully stylized low-poly city with skyscrapers, roads, cars, trees, streetlights and small moving traffic. Let me orbit, pan and zoom the camera.

Include four selectable powers: meteor strike, tornado, earthquake and laser beam.

Each ability must have unique visual effects, satisfying destruction animations, debris, particles, screen shake and sound effects. Buildings should visibly break apart or collapse when hit, not simply disappear.

Include a destruction percentage meter, a reset button and smooth interactions.

Prioritize a polished, responsive, playable experience over decorative UI. Build and run the complete app, fix runtime errors, and don't use placeholder buttons or fake interactions.
```
