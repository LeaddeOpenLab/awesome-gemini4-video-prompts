## Prompt (English)

```text
subject_definitions:
<Subject 1> is the model in <Picture 1>, including their face, skin tone, hairstyle, hair color, body proportions, and outfit exactly as shown.
<Subject 2> is the wearable product in <Picture 2>, including its exact silhouette, proportions, colors, materials, surface finish, and any printed label or logo design exactly as they appear in the image.

summary:
[reference generation] The target video is a 15-second cinematic fashion commercial in which <Subject 1> wears <Subject 2>. It is built as eight 1.5-second scenes followed by a 3-second hero finale. Each scene changes location, lighting, and composition on the beat, and in each scene one short word exists physically inside the environment. The finale is a full-frame hero shot in front of giant illuminated letters spelling "QUIVRE", with the tagline "Carry your own light." and the call to action "SHOP NOW".

retention_analysis:
<Subject 1> (appears in [Shot 2], [Shot 4], [Shot 5], [Shot 6], [Shot 7], [Shot 8], [Shot 9]): fully_preserved - the face, skin tone, hairstyle, hair color, body proportions, and outfit from <Picture 1> stay identical throughout; if <Subject 2> is a clothing item, footwear, or accessory, it replaces only the matching item of that outfit.
<Subject 2> (appears in [Shot 1] to [Shot 9]): fully_preserved - the shape, proportions, colors, materials, surface finish, and logo from <Picture 2> stay identical in every shot.

detailed_description:
The target video is a photoreal cinematic fashion commercial shot at blue hour and night, with low-key lighting, strong backlight and rim light, drifting atmospheric haze, wet reflective surfaces, deep contrast, and a color scheme of cool steel-blue shadows against the warm glow of <Subject 2>'s own dominant color. <Subject 1> is the only person, and <Subject 2> is the only featured product, worn naturally and always clearly visible and in focus. Every word of text exists physically inside the scene and is spelled exactly as written; it never covers the face or the product, and no other text appears.
[Shot 1] Total darkness filled with slow-moving haze. A narrow horizontal blade of white light sweeps across the frame and projects the word "QUIVRE" in thin, widely spaced capital letters onto a dark concrete wall. As the blade passes, it catches <Subject 2> worn by <Subject 1> in the foreground in a tight close-up, rim-lighting its edges and igniting its warm color. The camera pushes in slowly. A deep bass hit lands with the light sweep.
[Shot 2] At 00:01.500, the shot cuts to a long concrete tunnel at blue hour with a blinding white backlight at its far end and haze in the air. <Subject 1>, wearing <Subject 2>, walks out of the backlight from silhouette toward the camera, passing between enormous freestanding concrete letters spelling "NEW" that stand on both sides of the path. The camera tracks backward at a low angle. Heavy footsteps echo.
[Shot 3] At 00:03.000, the shot cuts to an extreme macro of <Subject 2> on the body of <Subject 1> in a dark room. A hard spotlight passes through a metal stencil and casts the word "DETAIL" as sharp letter-shaped light onto the dark wall behind, while the camera glides across the product's material texture and edges in raking light.
[Shot 4] At 00:04.500, the shot cuts to a windswept rooftop at dusk under a deep blue sky. Behind <Subject 1>, a huge white fabric banner printed with the black word "FREE" billows and snaps violently in the wind. Their hair whips across their face as they turn back toward the camera, <Subject 2> clearly visible. The camera circles at a low angle. Wind roars and fabric cracks.
[Shot 5] At 00:06.000, the shot cuts to a rain-soaked city crosswalk at night. Large white road-painted letters reading "EVERY DAY" lie across the wet asphalt, glistening with reflected streetlights and blurred traffic lights. <Subject 1> strides across the letters while the camera tracks alongside at the height of <Subject 2>, the painted words sliding through the foreground. Water splashes softly under each step.
[Shot 6] At 00:07.500, the shot cuts to a tall glass wall in a dark modern building lit by cold blue light from outside. The word "LIGHT" is etched large into the glass in the foreground. Behind the glass, <Subject 1> walks from left to right with <Subject 2> visible through the clear parts of the glass, their figure passing behind the etched letters. The camera slides sideways, creating strong parallax between the letters and the model.
[Shot 7] At 00:09.000, the shot cuts to a dark studio with haze. Enormous three-dimensional letters spelling "BOLD" stand behind <Subject 1>, sculpted from the same material and color as <Subject 2> and lit by one hard top light that throws deep shadows. <Subject 1> holds a still, confident full-body pose in front of them, wearing <Subject 2> on the body exactly as in the other shots; <Subject 2> is large, sharply lit, and never hidden or left out. The camera snap-zooms in on the beat. A punchy whoosh-and-hit accent lands.
[Shot 8] At 00:10.500, the shot cuts to a close-up of <Subject 1>'s face together with <Subject 2> beside a night window. Outside, a warm illuminated sign spelling "YOURS" is reflected in the window glass next to their face, blurred by raindrops. <Subject 1> looks directly into the camera with a calm, confident expression. The camera pushes in very slowly. Rain taps softly on the glass.
[Shot 9] At 00:12.000, the shot cuts to a newly staged, full-frame wide shot of a vast dark studio that fills the entire frame edge to edge, with no black bars, borders, or framed picture. Giant freestanding illuminated letters spelling "QUIVRE" glow warm white across the back of the set, reflected on the glossy black floor. <Subject 1> stands slightly right of center, large in the frame from head to knees and filling about two-thirds of the frame height, in a composed hero pose while wearing <Subject 2>, which is sharply lit by a spotlight and clearly readable as the hero product. In the empty lower-left area of the frame, clean white text "Carry your own light." fades in, then "SHOP NOW" appears inside a thin rounded outline button beneath it, never over the model's body. The camera stays locked off with no zoom or push-in so the whole face always stays in frame, and the final half second holds completely still like a printed advertising key visual. A final bass hit rings out with a long shimmering tail.

overall_soundscape:
Cinematic, close foley throughout, with whooshes on every cut and a low room hum under the finale. No voices or narration.

non_diegetic_music:
A dark, stylish electronic track at about 80 BPM with a deep sub-bass, a hard kick and snap, metallic percussion, and tense pulsing synths. Every cut lands on every second beat. It builds from [Shot 4], peaks at [Shot 7], then drops to silence for a breath before a final huge bass hit at 00:12.000 and a long reverb tail.
```
