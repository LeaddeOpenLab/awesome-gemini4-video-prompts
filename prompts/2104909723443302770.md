## Prompt (English)

```text
Create a 30-second video in a single HTML file using Three.js, where the camera continuously zooms in without cuts from the tip of a pencil on a desk.

[Zoom Flow (Continuous Single Shot)]
1. A sharpened hexagonal wooden pencil on a desk (no logos or text)
2. Tip of the lead: A cluster of shiny black particles mixing graphite and clay
3. Graphite particle: Cross-section showing multiple thin layers stacked together
4. Single layer: Hexagonal honeycomb lattice mesh of carbon atoms
5. Finally stops on a single carbon atom

[Staging]
- To make it clear that the initial pencil hexagon and final carbon hexagon share the same shape, briefly overlay the hexagonal outline of the pencil cross-section near the end
- Display short Japanese labels at the bottom of the screen at each stage ("鉛筆", "芯", "黒鉛の粒", "黒鉛の層", "炭素の六角形", "炭素原子"). Make them readable on mobile
- Display a current scale bar on the right side of the screen, transitioning through cm -> mm -> µm -> nm
- Use a calm, dark background, giving the graphite a metallic sheen and softly glowing carbon lattice

[Specifications]
- Do not use external images, 3D models, or asset files; draw all shapes, textures, and lighting entirely in code
- Use a square aspect ratio, autoplay immediately upon opening, pause on the atom for 1 second at the end, and then loop back to the start
- Load Three.js from cdnjs
```
