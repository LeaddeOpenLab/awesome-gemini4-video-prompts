## Prompt (English)

```text
[Identity Lock | Woman]
Adult female, ~28, straight black hair, fair skin, dark brown eyes; knee-length bright red wool coat, black jeans, black ankle boots; red coat is the dominant color; continuous single actor, no face-swapping or outfit changes.

[Identity Lock | Man]
Adult male, ~30, short dark brown hair, light stubble, light olive skin; navy jacket, grey hoodie, dark jeans; holding two white paper coffee cups without logos; continuous single actor, no face-swapping or outfit changes.

[Space]
Fixed geography: ~15m wide two-lane urban street flanked by storefronts; warm-lit cafe on left with glass door facing street; glass bus stop on right facing cafe; street extends to horizon; meteor streaks from top right to horizon, exploding outside city; dust wall surges towards camera; he exits cafe on left, she waits at bus stop on right, meeting in street center without screen direction inversion; ~10 pedestrians.

[First Frame]
24mm low-angle wide shot looking down street; quiet night street, shop lights, streetlamps, dark sky; top-right sky glows bright orange-white.

[Timeline / SHOT 1]
0.0–4.0s | 24mm low wide upward tilt: 0.0–1.5s sky lights up brighter than day, cast shadows swing rapidly; 1.5–3.0s pedestrians gaze up as massive burning meteor streaks across top-right sky towards horizon; 3.0–4.0s meteor glare reflects on cafe door glass, creating first match cut.

[Timeline / SHOT 2]
4.0–9.0s | Match cut from glare, 35mm handheld outside cafe: 4.0–5.5s he pushes door open holding two coffees, looks up and halts; 5.5–7.0s he sees her across the street at the bus stop, eyes meet; 7.0–8.0s both sprint into street; 8.0–9.0s massive white explosion at horizon washes out screen, creating second match cut.

[Timeline / SHOT 3]
9.0–15.0s | Match cut from whiteout, 35mm elevated street view: 9.0–11.0s whiteout fades to mushroom cloud and orange fireball; 11.0–13.5s shockwave blasts storefront windows successively towards camera, alarms blare, dust wall rolls forward; both sprint towards street center; 13.5–15.0s nearest window shatters toward lens, creating third match cut.

[Timeline / SHOT 4]
15.0–19.0s | Match cut from flying glass, 50mm eye-level profile, both in street center: 15.0–17.0s reaching out, he drops both coffees, liquid spills, glass whirls, dust wall 20m away, wind pulls hair; 17.0s time abruptly freezes completely; 17.0–19.0s absolute standstill except she slowly blinks, creating fourth match cut.

[Timeline / SHOT 5]
19.0–25.0s | Match cut from blink, 35mm handheld over her right shoulder: 19.0–20.5s realizing she can move, she looks around; 20.5–23.5s steps forward through suspended shards, gently deflects a floating glass shard which stays in place; passes frozen coffee spill, frozen pigeon, frozen pedestrians, frozen fiery dust wall; 23.5–25.0s approaches him and reaches out, creating fifth match cut.

[Timeline / SHOT 6]
25.0–30.0s | Match cut from reaching hand, 50mm close profile: 25.0–26.5s fingertips touch his frozen cheek; 26.5–28.0s he blinks, inhales, looks at her and unfreezes, clasping her hand; 28.0–30.0s adjacent suspended glass shard vibrates and drops; both watch it fall; scene cuts mid-fall.

[Physics]
Meteor illumination realistically shifts street shadows; shockwave shatters glass sequentially outward; dust wall is volumetric fire/dust; time freeze is total for all background elements with zero motion blur or drift; deflected shard stays at new position; gravity resumes on moving subjects; only final shard vibrates and drops.

[Cinematography]
6 sequential camera setups; 5 match cuts matched to ending elements (reflection, flash, shard, blink, hand); camera static during freeze except Shot 5 tracking; no slow motion, no hero low-angles.

[Lighting/Color]
Opens cold blue with warm shop light; switches to harsh orange-white under meteor; orange-red explosion lighting; frozen world back-lit by fire inside dust wall; red coat remains focal point.

[Audio]
Shot 1 quiet traffic to escalating roar; Shot 2 doorbell, footsteps, distant impact; Shot 3 blast roar, shattering windows, alarms; Shot 4 wind/glass noise abruptly cuts to dead silence at 17s; Shot 5 quiet footsteps on glass and soft breath; Shot 6 sharp inhale and gentle glass hum.

[Locks]
Strictly 6 shots, 5 match cuts; zero spoken dialogue; continuous outfits and character identities without fusion; exact anatomical consistency; live-action film realism; video cuts while the shard falls.
```
