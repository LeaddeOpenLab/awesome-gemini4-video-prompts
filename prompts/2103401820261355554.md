# INKBOUND Pixel Runner Game Prompt

![Preview by @agentgamesbot](<../data/gifs/2103401820261355554.gif>)

## Prompt (English)

````text
Using Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas 2D and the Web Audio API, create **INKBOUND**: one self-contained HTML file in which a sumi-e ink painting comes alive as a 16-bit pixel-art runner.

A tiny ink-drop spirit races along a black brush stroke that a painter's hand paints in real time. A rival painter attacks in indigo, and the spirit survives by **jumping, sinking into the ink, absorbing small ink and stepping back**.

- No external assets, libraries, fonts, audio files or network requests. All art, text and sound are generated in code.
- The page boots into a self-playing attract demo that loops seamlessly. Any control input starts a playable run.

## 1. Rendering
- Draw everything into a `Uint8Array` of palette indices at a fixed **160x90**. Convert it to RGBA once per frame.
- Copy that frame to a full-window canvas at the **largest integer scale** that fits, centred, with black margins. Use `imageSmoothingEnabled = false` and CSS `image-rendering: pixelated`. Account for `devicePixelRatio`.
- Integer pixels only. No anti-aliasing, gradients, `shadowBlur` or rotation. Slanted lines are pixel stair-steps, and gradients are Bayer-dithered bands.
- Simulate at a fixed **60 Hz** with an accumulator, and render with `requestAnimationFrame`.
- Zero allocation in the loop: typed arrays, fixed pools and pre-built text arrays.

| Paper / dusk sky | `#ece4d6 #e2d3bf #d9bda3 #c9a58e` | sky bands, paper |

| Hands (CC0 sprite) | `#572020 #ffce96 #c2675b #ffe4ab #de9176 #f5bb8c` | hands only |

## 3. Scene
- **Sky**: dithered bands from pale paper at the top to peach dusk at the horizon (y = 44), with paper-fibre specks and a pale sun.
- **Mountains**: two parallax ridges of flat washes on 512 px repeating height strips. Each has a one-shade-darker ridge line, small wash-coloured pine silhouettes and a thin dithered mist band.
- **Speed streaks**: wash-coloured streaks whose count and length grow with speed.
- **Road**: an 8–9 px black brush stroke, top at y ≈ 46 with a gentle sine undulation (±3 px).
  - Ragged dry-brush edges and bristle streaks in `#272523`.
  - Ink drips fall from its underside.
  - It turns dry and streaky over the last 16 px before the painter's brush at x = 138, because it is being painted right now.

..BBBBBBB...                  ...B....B...

- **Scarf**: 7–9 px of seal red streaming behind with a 2-frame flutter. It lies flat on the ink while sinking.
- **Pose registers**: bounce, lean, squash and stretch (repeat or drop one or two body rows), widening and a 1 px eye shift.
  - Ease the registers, but quantize them on a ~12 fps clock so the motion reads as hand-animated sprites.
  - Take-off, landing and each action snap to their pose instantly.
- Draw the eyes in a second pass so body pixels never overwrite them.

- **Brush**: 4 px wide and the **same width from handle to tip**. From the tip:
  - 2 rows 2 px wide, then 2 more soaked rows (ink `#121110`, or indigo `#1c2a78` for the rival, with a light left edge)
  - 4 rows of light hair (`#efd6a0` with a `#a8844c` right edge)
  - 2 rows of gold band
  - 4 rows of black lacquer handle going into the fist
- **Painter**:
  - The fist sits at the bottom right, holding the brush straight up. The brush tip touches the underside of the road at x = 138, and the hand bobs 1 px.
  - A white robe sleeve (with a `#e2d3bf` fold, a `#a39a90` rim and a `#572020` outline) runs off the bottom edge.
  - Over a gap, the painter lifts the brush 6 px and drips.
- **Rival**: the same fist flipped vertically, brush pointing down, with an indigo sleeve (`#3150c8`, `#1c2a78` shade, `#9fb6ff` rim) running off the top edge. The eraser uses the pointing finger, flipped to point down.

## 6. Actions (play-mode controls in brackets)
- **Jump** [W / Space / ↑]:
  - Short wind-up squash (4 ticks in the demo, 2 in play), then launch at vy −3.4 with gravity 0.2.
  - Stretch on the way up, curl at the apex, and land with a squash plus an ink splash.
- **Double jump** [W again in the air]: the spirit bursts into **10 ink dots orbiting its eyes** (one dot red) for 9 ticks, hanging in place, then re-forms and relaunches at vy −3.0.
- **Sink** [S, hold]:
  - The spirit melts into the road. Use the 3-row SINK shape drawn 1 px lower, so only the crown and eyes show above the ink.
  - Ripples spread on the ink surface. It lasts at least 24 ticks, or as long as S is held.
  - Emerge with a splash on release. Sinking over a gap falls. Jumping from a sink is allowed.
- **Absorb** [D]:
  - Opens a rotating ink vortex in front of the spirit for 14 ticks in the demo, 28 in play.
  - Any small or medium arrow or flicked droplet that comes within 1.5 px is sucked in. Its indigo particles turn to ink on the way, the spirit swells and a white **ABSORB** seal with red text stamps.
  - Each absorb extends the window by 5 ticks (max 30). A miss gives a 12-tick cooldown.
  - The giant bolt, strokes and blots cannot be absorbed.
- **Back step** [A]:
  - Bursts backwards (dx starts at −3.6 and decays ×0.8 for 10 ticks, clamped at −20 px), leaving fading afterimages.
  - Holds for 24 ticks in play (18 in the demo), then drifts back at 0.45 px/tick.
- **Early-press assist**: in play, the correct move pressed up to **24 ticks early** is held and fired on that attack's ideal tick, so good players always get last-moment dodges.

## 7. Threats (all indigo)
- **Arrows**: small (9 px), medium (13 px) and a **giant bolt** (24 px, which shakes the screen as it passes). They fly in diagonally from the top right.
  - Each has a stair-stepped shaft, a head, fletching, a light top rim and a dotted trail.
  - An arrow that hits the road thunks in and quivers, then blinks out.
  - In play, a small blinking indigo "!" seal marks where an off-screen arrow will enter.
- **Flicked droplets**: 3x3 indigo drops. They are aimed from the rival's brush tip at the top right toward the spirit's head height, and splash when they hit the road.
- **Rival strokes**: the rival hand drops in at the right edge while a blinking dotted indigo guide shows the lane.
  - It then **sweeps right to left at 20 px/tick**. Its brush lays a 4–5 px indigo stroke with a light top rim, dark streaks and a splatter trail.
  - The stroke stays harmful for its burn time (capped at 19 ticks in play). Then it dries away through dithered holes over 12 ticks while the hand lifts off.
  - Lanes are screen-space lines at a height above the road that follows the ground under the hero. A stroke can have a slope and x-limits.
- **Blot**: the rival hand hangs at the top right while a drop swells on its brush tip.
  - When the target spot scrolls under it, the drop falls in 8 ticks and splats. It spreads into a puddle of radius 9 on the road (2–3 px tall, rim `#9fb6ff`).
  - It holds, then dries from 40 to 70 ticks after landing.
- **Eraser**: the pointing finger hovers while a dotted indigo outline marks a stretch of road.
  - When that spot reaches x ≈ 132, the finger rubs, growing a 60 px **gap** in the road (±3 px per tick) and scattering paper crumbs.
- The hands never hurt. Only ink on the paper does.

## 8. Attack patterns (ticks at 60 Hz; L = 0 in the demo and 32 in play)
| # | Attack | Answer | Demo script (spawn → dodge) | Length |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Medium arrow at head height | Absorb | t 20 spawn, arrive t 68+L → absorb t 64 | 110 |
| 1 | Rival flicks 4 droplets (t 20, 23, 26, 29) | Absorb | arrive ~t 64+L → absorb t 59 | 120 |
| 2 | Low stroke just above the road | Jump | t 20 warn (25+L ticks), burn 27 → jump t 46 | 115 |
| 3 | High stroke at head height | Sink | t 20 warn (25+L), burn 29 → sink t 46 for 36 | 120 |
| 4 | Blot on the road | Jump | t 10 start, spirit reaches its centre at t 70+L → jump t 59 | 130 |
| 5 | Eraser gap + medium arrow | Jump, then double jump over the arrow | t 12 start, gap reached t 58+L; arrow t 47 → jump t 52, double t 77 | 170 |
| 6 | X of two diagonal strokes centred on the spirit (arms from 22 to 2 px above the road) | Sink | warn 25+L and 29+L → sink t 44 for 44 | 125 |
| 7 | Giant bolt aimed at mid-body | Back step | t 20 spawn, arrive t 70+L → back step t 60 | 130 |
| 8 | Chapter end | – | t 0 red **END** seal + gong; t 20–49 a wooden scroll rod rolls right → left over blank paper showing **CHAPTER N** in ink with a red number and a dry-brush underline; t 50 reset the world (next chapter); t 50–79 unroll left → right | 80 |

- **Demo**: the patterns play in order 0 → 8 forever, and every dodge happens at the **last possible moment**.
- Seed every random choice from a fixed seed at each chapter reset, and reset every scroll offset, so every demo chapter is pixel-identical (one chapter ≈ 19.0 s).
- The demo starts on the unrolling scroll.

## 9. Close call, hits and judgment
- Each tick, measure the real gap between the spirit's box and each threat:
  - arrows: sample the front 60 % of the shaft and subtract a radius
  - strokes: per column
  - blot: box-to-box
- **Close call**: fires at the closest approach when the minimum gap is **≥ 0 and ≤ 4 px** (≤ 10 in play). A blot is judged only once the spirit has fully passed it.
- On a close call:
  - freeze for 5 ticks
  - for 18 ticks the whole background turns into a **pencil sketch**: region edges in `#857d76` on blank paper, light hatching on the mountains, fading out with Bayer dither
  - a red **CLOSE CALL** seal stamps above the spirit, first drawn larger for an impact frame. On the title screen, stamp it below the spirit so it never covers the title
- **An attack judged as dodged can never hurt afterwards.**
- **Hit (play)**: an arrow must reach 1 px into the spirit's box, and a stroke or blot 2 px. Falling into a gap also costs a life and bounces the spirit back up.
  - A hit costs one ink-drop life, triggers a 7-tick freeze, a shake and red sparks, then 110 ticks of blinking invulnerability.
  - In play, the giant bolt lands 3 px further ahead than in the demo, so a back step always clears it.

## 10. Play mode
- **Start**:
  - Any control rolls the scroll over the demo into "CHAPTER 1 / READY".
  - The HUD shows a red score seal, a combo "x2…x9", "BEST" with the best score, a small red seal tally (close calls + absorbs) and three ink-drop lives.
- **Chapter 1**: the fixed order. A **hint seal** above the spirit names the answer ("D ABSORB", "HOLD S", "W JUMP", "W AGAIN", "A BACK"; button names on touch devices). It appears 44 ticks before the ideal moment, turns red and blinks in the last 10 ticks, and vanishes once the right key is pressed.
- **Chapter 2+**: shuffle the 8 attacks and add one extra random attack per chapter (9, 10, … capped at 16). The same attack never comes twice in a row. Chapters are endless.
- **Pace**: speed starts at 3 px/tick and rises 0.15 per chapter (capped at 4.0). A cleared chapter refills one life (max 3).
- **Score**:
  - distance (speed × 0.05 per tick)
  - +100 × combo per close call or absorb (the combo resets on a hit)
  - +500 × level on each new chapter
- **Best score**: kept in `localStorage` inside try/catch. It is saved the moment it is beaten (every 0.5 s), when the page is hidden or closed, and at game over.
- **Game over**: "THE INK DRIED", the score, and "NEW RECORD!" if the best at the start was beaten. Press to retry; after 15 s idle, return to the demo.

## 11. Input and devices
- **Keyboard**: W/Space/↑ jump, A/← back, S/↓ sink (hold), D/→ absorb, M mute, H hides the title in the demo.
- **Embedded use (https://t.co/1pYR66UQCe iframe)**:
  - On pointerdown, call `window.focus()`, and only `preventDefault()` for touch pointers, or the iframe never gets keyboard focus.
  - Hints are per device: keyboard users always see "W JUMP · A BACK · S SINK · D ABSORB" and "PRESS SPACE TO START". Touch users see "USE THE BUTTONS / OR TAP AND SWIPE" and "TAP TO START". Switch modes on the first key or touch.
- **Touch**: draw pixel-style buttons (ink body, grey rim, turning red while pressed, labels in the same 3x5 pixel font) **in the black margins outside the game image**, so they never cover it.
  - **Portrait / tall screens**: centre the game above a row of four big buttons: BACK, SINK, ABSORB, JUMP.
  - **Landscape**: BACK and SINK on the left, ABSORB and JUMP on the right, and a small mute button.
  - If the margins are too small, drop the integer scale by one.
  - Buttons act on press (sink while held) and support multi-touch.
  - Tap = jump and swipes (down, right, left) still work in the game area.

## 12. Sound (synthesized, starts on the first input; M mutes)
- Pre-render every sound into buffers with deterministic noise.
- **Music**: exactly **19.0 s** (32 beats ≈ 101 BPM), looping in sync with the demo chapter.
  - An A-minor-pentatonic **koto melody** (Karplus-Strong plucks with DC removed), 8 bars of eighth notes.
  - A plucked bass on beats 1 and 3.
  - Taiko hits (a pitch-dropping sine plus a noise thump) and woodblock clicks.
  - A soft 16th-note shaker.
  - A breathing A2/E3/A3 drone with integer cycles per loop.
  - Note tails wrap around the loop end, so it is seamless.
- **Effects** (one per event):
  - Jump: a pluck with an upward chirp. Double jump: rising plucks and a shimmer.
  - Land: a soft thud. Sink: a downward bloop with bubbles. Emerge: a splash.
  - Back step: a whoosh. Absorb: an inhale when it opens, then a gulp and a chime.
  - Arrow: a whoosh as it enters the screen and a thunk when it sticks. Giant bolt: a heavy whoosh and a boom.
  - Droplet: a plip when flicked and a splash when it lands. Stroke: a soft "shh" warning and a sharp swish when it fires.
  - Blot: a plink when the drop falls and a splat when it lands. Eraser: a rub-rub.
  - Close call: a woodblock "tock" and a stamp thud. Chapter end: a gong, then paper rustles for the roll and the unroll.
  - Also: hit, game over and start.
- Mix through a compressor; the music sits below the effects.

## 13. Quality bar and self-check (do this before you finish)
- Crisp pixels at any size, a stable 60 fps (well under 2 ms per frame), and a silhouette you can read in the busiest frame.
- Run the demo headlessly for at least two chapters. Confirm:
  - zero collisions
  - every attack ends in a close call or an absorb
  - no console errors
  - two chapters render pixel-identical frames
- Simulate play with ideal inputs, pressing up to 24 ticks early, for 12 chapters of random order. Confirm no hits, and that doing nothing loses a life on every attack.
- With random timing, confirm no close call is ever awarded while overlapping and no hit ever follows a close call on the same attack.
- Show a screenshot of each attack's dodge.

*Hand sprites: "Pixel Hand Gesture Cursor Asset" by Lintnaya (CC0).*
````
