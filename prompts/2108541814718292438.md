## Prompt (English)

```text
one html file, three.js. zen garden seen from above. the sand is a canvas heightmap used as displacement + bump, raking with the mouse paints 5 grooves and 6 ridges along the path. 5 mossy rocks with raked rings around them, 2 stone lanterns with point lights, a red maple dropping leaves. a pill toggle for morning / golden / night. at golden the sun sits very low so every groove casts a shadow.
```
