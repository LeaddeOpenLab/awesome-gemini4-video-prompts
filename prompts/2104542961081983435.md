## Prompt (English)

```text
Hey Opus 5.5. 

Create a 90–120 second educational motion-graphics video in English about the current limitations of recursive self-evolving AI agents. 

Use a paper-cutout stop-motion style on a white background, with tiny square characters explaining large visualized concepts.

Juicy motion design such as anticipation, overshoot, squash and stretch, and paper tear, flip and slide transitions. 

Build it from dozens or even hundreds of paper elements generated with GPT Image 2.5 on Fal AI.

Before animating, study articles from the last three months on the limitations and open problems of self-evolving agents, and turn them into one engaging story.
 
Keep all on-screen text plain, specific and grammatically correct, and avoid AI-slop patterns: no "not X, but Y" constructions, no three-fragment lists, no rhetorical questions, no dramatic ellipses
```
