# Code-Animated Short Film: Xi the Mechanical Hermit Crab

## Prompt (English)

```text
You will act as director, animator, rigger, compositor, sound designer, and rendering engineer responsible for producing a 45–50 second pure code-animated short film. The goal is to "look like a genuine professional studio short and go viral on Platform X". Treat this project as a multi-stage production process. Do not rush to complete the final render. Set phased milestones, continuously render still frames, examine them closely, offer honest critique, and make revisions.

0. The Film in One Sentence

A mechanical hermit crab named "Xi", the deep sea's last "lamp lighter", carries a glowing pearl night after night across the seabed searching for lost creatures in need of guidance. On this night, it journeys across six distinctly different deep-sea secret realms chasing a flickering patch of light. When it finally catches up to the light source and discovers it is merely the reflection of its own pearl, the camera pulls back—the entire ocean is actually a snow globe on a desk, currently being gently shaken by a hand; the "snowflakes" inside are suspended fluorescent flakes. This is not a loop narrative, but a quiet, relieved epiphany.

The story must require zero dialogue. Xi conveys emotion purely through the pulsating frequency of the pearl, the opening/closing of its claws, and the rhythm of releasing bubbles. Every 3 to 5 seconds, a new visual surprise must appear.

1. Character Design (Non-negotiable)

Before writing any code, design and document the character in detail in docs/character_bible.md (no reference images; design from scratch ensuring internal consistency):

Dimensions: Approximately 18 cm wide (excluding claws), one of the smallest creatures across the six realms; the whole film must reflect its tininess and loneliness via the massive scale of surrounding scenery.

Shell: Not a natural crab shell, but a bionic shell repurposed from a discarded brass pocket watch case, covered with dents from deep-sea high pressure and barnacle/coral encrustations, with rusted gear patterns around the shell rim.

Eyes: Not conventional crab eyes, but two round dark-blue glass lenses on the front of the shell with tiny coral-like bioluminescent filaments inside. Emotions are expressed via filament density, color temperature (warm/cool), and flicker rate, rather than traditional pupil dilation.

Core Prop — The Pearl: A fist-sized glowing pearl steadily shielded by the larger right claw, its lifelong sole beacon and "heart". The pearl's light pulses like breathing alongside Xi's emotions, serving as the most vital emotional anchor of the film.

Claws: Two mechanical claws, one large and one small. The large claw (right) exclusively cradles the pearl and never releases it; the small claw (left) handles all interactions, pointing, touching, and defense, exceptionally agile.

Walking Legs: Six slender articulated legs with a wave-like alternating gait (non-synchronized stepping), each step sinking briefly into seabed sediments and lifting gently, kicking up tiny clusters of suspended particles.

Personality: Silent, determined, possessing a gentle resilience born of "having seen too much darkness to not cherish light". It is not fleeing, but actively seeking.

Extract an exact palette from your color design into src/theme/palette.ts: deep sea indigo (background tone), oxidized brass green (shell), pearl milky-white & warm gold (beacon light), coral fluorescent cyan (bioluminescent elements), deep ochre (seabed sediment).

Identity Lock Rule: Across every realm, Xi's pocket watch silhouette, dual claw proportion disparity, six-legged gait rhythm, and pearl position/pose must remain instantly recognizable. Realms alter lighting atmosphere and ecological textures, but never its core anatomy.

2. Tech Stack

Remotion (React + TypeScript) for frame-accurate, deterministic rendering. Output resolution 1920×1080, 30fps, H.264 encoding, high bitrate (CRF ~14). Landscape mode.

Character skeleton and primary art assets in SVG format. Particles, currents, glow, bubbles, and suspended sediments in Canvas/WebGL (@remotion/three or raw Canvas).

SVG filters (feTurbulence, feDisplacementMap, feGaussianBlur, feColorMatrix) and custom shaders for water refraction, bioluminescent glow, and pressure deformation.

@remotion/motion-blur (or custom sub-frame accumulation) for motion blur during rapid swimming and current sweeps.

Python (numpy/scipy/PIL) for palette generation, procedural underwater audio synthesis, and QA contact sheets. ffmpeg for muxing, loop verification, and contact sheet generation.

Everything seeded for deterministic rendering; no unseeded Math.random().

No external copyrighted assets or real brand logos. Fonts restricted to Google Fonts.

Target hardware: Reasonably evaluate execution environment resources and keep render concurrency balanced.

3. Character Rigging (Build this first; everything hinges on it)

Build src/character/ as a 2D cutout model:

Hierarchy: Root → 6-leg walking mechanism (independent phase shift per leg for wave gait) → Shell body → Large claw arm (shoulder → forearm → pincer, socketed for pearl) → Small claw arm (shoulder → forearm → fine pincer, interchangeable poses: touch, point, defensive open, caress pearl) → Eyestalks (two independently swiveling stalks, each with a glowing lens eye).

Lighting System (Most Critical): Pearl, eye lenses, and light seeping from shell seams form an interconnected emotional illumination system. Parameters include: pearl pulse frequency & amplitude, eye filament density & color temp, shell seam light leakage intensity, overall glow radius, special states (frantic flashing = alert, soft diffused glow = safe, filaments collapsing into a single tight line = hyper-focused stare, pearl dimming out then reigniting = rallying after emotional low).

Expression/State Targets: Curious (eyestalks lean forward, filaments spread in floating question-mark shapes), Alert (eyes narrow, pearl pulse quickens), Lonely (pearl aura shrinks and dims, gait slows), Delighted (pearl bursts with brightness, small claw gestures openly), Focused Pursuit (eyestalks locked ahead, six legs accelerate into sprint gait), Melancholy (instant of realizing the truth, filaments slowly relaxing into gentle arcs), Relieved (pearl aura steady and warm, six legs resting in stationary pose).

Secondary Action: Independent eyestalk motion (alternating scanning left/right when exploring), foot depression into sediment with dust kicks, subtle rhythmic tightening of the large claw guarding the pearl (like a heartbeat), slight tilt and recovery when hit by currents, intermittent bubble release from shell seam joints (breathing rhythm).

Rotation Views: Consistent front, 3/4 front, side, and rear views with clean perspective switching.

Action Loops: Wave-like forward walk, idle hover (subtle six-leg stepping + pearl breathing pulse + bubble release), alert retreat, looking up at light, clinging to rock face to evade, extending claw to touch unknown object.

Pearl Socket: Animatable grip angle, light creating subtle interactive reflections matching environment color temperature.

Stylized Skin Interface: Rigged prop skins able to adjust ambient light tone, water turbidity, glow intensity, and background creature silhouette density without changing geometry across the six realms.

Milestone 1: Render a "Lineup Sheet"—Xi's four rotation views and seven emotional states displayed side-by-side. Iterate until recognition and emotional delivery are unmistakable before proceeding.

4. Secret Realms (Realms switch along "deep-sea vertical ecological layers", not art styles or seasons)

Each realm is a full environment component containing foreground, midground, background parallax layers, and dedicated lighting/water skins. Build a shared world layer system so all realms share camera and depth logic.

Scale Rule: Camera stays close to Xi's eye level (~10cm off seabed). Corals appear like forests, shipwrecks like mountains, plankton light points like a starry sky.

Base Realm: Gloomy deep-sea plain. Sparse sediment, distant faint thermal vent glow, lonely and silent. Serves as starting point and final ocean setting before reveal.

Bioluminescent Jellyfish Swarm: Enormous translucent jellyfish floating lazily, rendered with subsurface scattering, colored in soft magenta and cyan-blue, tentacles trailing like glowing lanterns.

Hydrothermal Vent Ecology: Scalding black smokers erupting, tube worms swaying like red-and-white forests, stark contrast of fiery orange-red against deep black, continuous heat shimmer distortion.

Shipwreck Labyrinth: Rusted steel structures standing like skeletal giants, ochre rust intertwined with dark green algae, sharp Tyndall rays piercing fractured hulls, gothic yet not gloomy atmosphere.

Bioluminescent Algae Canyon: Canyon covered in bioluminescent algae, dreamlike blue-green glow along cliff faces, floating particle dust, ethereal purple and neon green tones.

Glacial Fissure: Eerie blue light shafts piercing thick surface ice overhead, ice crystals refracting prismatic highlights, minimalist icy blue-white-gray palette, cold and pure with suspended ice shards.

Emotional Core/Motif: The Pearl's Reflection Riddle. In every realm, a faint glow resembling the pearl flickers in the distance, beckoning Xi onward—in the jellyfish realm it's a jellyfish's glow; in vents, mineral crystal reflection; in shipwrecks, a porthole glass glint; in canyons, clustered fire-algae; in glaciers, refracted ice beams. Each chase ends in an empty-handed discovery, driving the suspense until the ultimate truth.

5. Signature Transition: "Current Sweep"

Realm transitions must immerse the viewer via "being swept into the next waters by a powerful current" rather than simple cuts. Build CurrentSweepTransition:

Streamlines and suspended particles surge in from one side, filling the frame (initially partially revealing Xi's silhouette).

Xi undergoes brief squashing/stretching deformation (dragged by current), eyestalks and legs struggling against flow, before the current recedes to reveal the new realm emerging from misty haze.

Each transition features a deep current roar, ending with Xi releasing a startled bubble pop.

Transitions last 8–12 frames. Vary flow direction and particle seed to prevent repetition.

6. Beat Sheet (~48s at 30fps)

Timing serves as reference; fine-tune for pacing. The first 2 seconds must hook immediately.

Act I: Glimmer (0:00–0:03)
Low-angle tracking shot, gloomy deep-sea plain base realm. Xi moves slowly guarding its pearl, eyestalks scanning warily. Ahead, an unfamiliar faint light flickers and vanishes. Begins mid-stride (for easy looping).
At 0:02, Xi sharply pivots toward the light; the first transition hits without warning.

Act II: Chasing the Light (0:03–0:14)
Rapid realm transitions (~1.4s each): Jellyfish Swarm → Hydrothermal Vents → Shipwreck Labyrinth → Algae Canyon → Glacial Fissure.
Emotion arc: Focused pursuit (locked gaze, accelerating pace), brief scare at the vent (swaying tube worms, evasive dodge), shifting to weary persistence (pearl dims slightly yet presses on), finally nearing the light source in the glacier, holding its breath.
Locked tracking camera framing; only surrounding realms morph to build mounting tension.

Act III: The Truth (0:14–0:30)
Inside the glacial fissure, the light is finally within reach—a glint refracted off a massive ice crystal. Xi reaches out with its small claw to touch it; the glint shatters and vanishes. Stunned, it slowly looks down at the pearl it has guarded all along, truly "seeing" it for the first time—the pearl's glow is identical to the phantom it chased.
Surrounding crystals begin softly resonating and glowing in response. Xi's tense eyestalk filaments slowly unfurl into gentle curves.

Act IV: Settling (0:30–0:38)
Xi gently unclasps the pearl from its large claw, cradling it before its chest with the small claw, watching it as bubbles release in slow, peaceful intervals. Not defeated, it shows quiet relief—the light it sought was beside it all along.
It safely nests the pearl back into the large claw, turns around, transitioning from a sprinting chase gait back into the leisurely wave-like walk of a lamp lighter, slowly wandering deeper into the seabed plain.

Act V: Freeze & Reveal (0:38–0:47)
Everything freezes: drifting ice crystal dust, Xi mid-turn. All audio abruptly ceases for ~1 second of absolute silence.
Camera slowly pulls back. A circular curved edge appears: the entire ocean realm is inside a glass snow globe on a desk. A human hand is gently holding the globe, with "snowflakes" drifting down—suspended fluorescent flakes identical to the light dust Xi pursued.
Outside the window is a deep blue night; warm lamplight falls on the glass surface, reflecting the silhouette of the tiny mechanical crab crawling within.

Act VI: Lingering Afterglow (0:47–0:50)
Camera slowly pushes in on the glass reflection, revealing the faint smiling outline of the viewer looking in.
No hard loop cut—the film ends with a slow fade-out on the pearl's warm glow. If a loopable version is needed, produce out/xi_loop_ambient.mp4 separately as an ambient loop seamlessly joining start and end via a slowly rotating globe shot.

7. Camera & Cinematography

Virtual camera: position, zoom, rotation, subtle floating drift, current impact shake pulses, forward push acceleration during chase, slow pullback for reveal.

Default low camera height emphasizing Xi's smallness. Shallow depth of field: blurred foreground sediments/coral, soft background like macro photography.

Depth: At least 4 parallax layers per realm. Underwater aerial perspective (turbidity increasing with distance, light falloff with depth).

Motion blur during rapid swimming and current sweeps.

Independent lighting per realm: key light (bioluminescence/ambient) / fill / rim via gradient overlays. Pearl and eye glow cast subtle warm reflections on nearby rock and Xi's shell.

Overall underwater cinematic texture: fine grain, subtle vignette, water chromatic aberration, slight color fringe on chase beats.

Composition: Place Xi along rule-of-thirds. Pearl's radiance must remain visible at thumbnail scale.

8. Text & Typography

No dialogue, no subtitles, no title cards. Narrative unfolds immediately.

No UI elements—pure ecological narrative relying on light, movement, and biological behavior.

9. Audio (Procedural Sound Design, Original Audio)

All audio generated via code (Python synthesis or offline Web Audio), mixed with ffmpeg:

Xi's Voice: Bubbles, shell friction, and gentle breathing hums of pearl pulsing—playful bubble streams when curious, rapid pops when alert, steady rhythmic breathing in pursuit, long relieved sigh bubble upon revelation, calm slow breath when at peace. Never vocalized.

Xi's Body: Subtle rustle of six legs in sediment, mechanical click of pincers, shell brushing rocks, faint crystal resonance as pearl pulses.

Music: Ethereal, cavernous underwater ambient score with instruments shifting by realm (glass harp for jellyfish, bass drums & brass for vents, string harmonics for shipwreck, glockenspiel for algae, vocal chant & ice percussion for glacier).

SFX: Low rumble of current transitions, crisp ice cracking, quiet room tone during reveal (distant clock ticking, night breeze outside window), lingering crystal chime as the pearl settles at the end.

Loudness normalized to approximately -14 LUFS. Stems saved to audio/stems/.

10. Production Workflow & Self-Improvement Loop

Execute in order, skipping no gates:

Planning: Author docs/character_bible.md, docs/shotlist.md (frame range, camera, emotion, pearl intensity, realm, audio per shot), and docs/style_guide.md. Present the shot list before proceeding.

Rigging: Build Xi; pass the Lineup gate (Section 3).

Realms: Build each realm as standalone stills with Xi. Render all 6 realms into a contact sheet. Verify recognition lock and scale.

Transitions: Build CurrentSweepTransition; render 5-second test.

Animatic: Assemble the film at 960×540 with rough motion and placeholder audio. Review and adjust pacing.

Full Animation: Polish (secondary motion, easing, timing, light breathing), finalize rendering (lighting, grain, blur, water texture).

Audio Pass & Mix.

Final Render.

For every shot, run this review cycle at least 3 times:

Render 3–5 keyframes and inspect thoroughly.

Score 1–10 on: Xi recognition, emotional clarity via light/body language, wordless narrative impact, composition, scale, depth, lighting, polish, thumbnail readability.

Log scores and top 3 flaws in docs/review_log.md, patch, re-render, rescore. Repeat until every category scores ≥8.

Generate ffmpeg contact sheet (1 frame every 0.5s) after every preview render to view the whole film sequence at a glance.

Be brutally honest in reviews. Common flaws: rigid eyestalks, sliding feet instead of stepping, glow looking like stickers rather than light sources, unbalanced water clarity, rushed emotional beats, scale errors.

11. Deliverables

out/xi_final_1080p.mp4: Final film.

out/xi_ambient_loop.mp4: Ambient looping version of the closing snow globe shot.

out/poster_frame.png: Best single thumbnail frame for Platform X (recommend Xi staring at the pearl reflection amidst ice crystal dust).

out/lineup.png: Xi lineup across all realms.

Clean, commented source code with a README on how to re-render.

Start from Step 1. Ask clarifying questions on major decisions before beginning, or make bold creative choices independently.
```
