## Prompt (English)

```text
Create a mini documentary explaining how a Transformer works, focusing on the Encoding stage (of the input prompt) and Decoding (output generation). You can use Portuguese labels and generated sounds simulating a machine in operation. Use camera movement to show the process across its stages. Represent the Transformer as a large vertical machine, with processing going from the top (input context) to the base (output context).
```
