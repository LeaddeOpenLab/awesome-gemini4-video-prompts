## Prompt (English)

```text
Build a single-file WebGPU "material study" page called "Plush Hedgehog" in my plush studio style: editorial layout, large italic serif title "Plush / Hedgehog.", kicker "Material Studies / No. 021", caption "A coat of soft felted spines, a button nose, four little paws. Curls up when it is shy.", live status chip, specimen panel on the right (tools, fur palettes, sliders, action buttons), stats row (Mass, Volume, Steps, Curls), collapsible "Inside the experiment" notes. UI in English, works on mobile.

The toy: a round plush hedgehog — one SDF body (a round bun with a pointed snout, two little round ears, a flat seat) meshed with surface nets, a button nose, glass eyes, four short plush paws (tubes with a bendable wire inside that keep the shape you bend them to).

Physics: XPBD soft body on a hex lattice (shape-matched cells + tetrahedral volume preservation, live volume readout), skinned to the mesh, sitting under gravity on Coulomb friction. It breathes with its flanks, twitches its snout when it sniffs, and slowly rights itself toward the way it last walked. Curling into a ball is a second rest shape found once at load (the body led nose-to-tail and squeezed into a sphere so the stuffing can really take that shape); curling blends every cell's rest shape and every tet's rest volume toward it. Nothing holds the ball upright, so a push makes it roll; when it stops it unrolls and stands up again.

Spines: hundreds of soft felted cones rooted on the skin, rebuilt every frame from the skin's stretch and turn; they lie back toward the tail, stand up when it bristles or curls, lag behind every jolt on a damped spring, part around the finger and fold on the table instead of going through it. Cream root, dark band, pale tip, a short fuzz over each. The face, belly and paws wear minky shell fur that lies back from the nose and takes the comb.

Behaviours: Trundle (walks on its paws in diagonal pairs), Curl up, Roll, Bristle, Smooth fur, Reset. Hand: pick it up — hold it a moment and it curls into a prickly ball in your hand; put it down, squash it, throw it; bend a paw. Finger: it trundles over and sniffs your finger; run the finger through the spines and they part; poke its back and it huffs and rolls up. Comb: brush the minky. Sliders: Stuffing, Pile, Spines. Palettes: Hazel, Ash, Blush. ¼ speed and mesh toggles. 60 fps.
```
