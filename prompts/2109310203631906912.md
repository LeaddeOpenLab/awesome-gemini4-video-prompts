## Prompt (English)

```text
Add hover animations to the side navigation icons, on both active and inactive items. Each icon should get its own short animation that fits its meaning, plays once, and ends in the icon's normal pose. Iterate until each animation scores at least 9/10 for smoothness, clarity, subtlety and consistency with the others.
```
