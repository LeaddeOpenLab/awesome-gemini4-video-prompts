## Prompt (English)

```text
Create the most impressive explosion effect you can and an original scene to support it. Choose the scale, environment, and visual language yourself. Ensure the initiation of the explosion, the propagation of force, and the aftermath it leaves behind are observable; a single-frame flash or random particle shower is not enough. Allow the user to restart it and view its impact from different angles.

The creative choices are yours: do not constrain the scene, art direction, camera, mechanics, technology, and interaction into predetermined conventional templates. Do not settle for the first run-of-the-mill web demo that comes to mind: first briefly weigh a few ideas within your domain, choose the unique one that will look strongest on video, and then turn it into a working product. Decorative imitation that makes the subject look easier than it is will not be accepted. Cinematic and aesthetic choices should be visible in the final result.
```
