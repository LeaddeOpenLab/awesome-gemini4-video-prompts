## Prompt (English)

```text
Make a 15-second motion-design showreel, like a résumé portfolio reel for a motion designer named CLAUDE. Go all out.
- 1920×1080, 60 fps, exactly 15 s. Every frame and every sound is generated in code.
- 128 BPM, 8 bars; each section is one bar (1.875 s), and every move lands on a beat.
- Palette: ink #0C0C10, bone #F2EEE6, red #FF3B1D, cobalt #2E4BFF, lime #D8FF3C. Fonts: Bahnschrift (variable), Noto Sans TC, Consolas.
- A red dot is the recurring hero and ends as the period in "CLAUDE."
Sections: 00 dot bounces with squash & stretch → stretches into a line that splits the screen ·
01 kinetic type MOTION / DESIGN (spring mask, red bar wipe, variable-weight ripple, marquee, push into the "O") ·
02 17×9 shape grid (radial waves: circle → square → Truchet arcs → spiral collapse) ·
03 GLSL-raymarched red blob (sphere → metaballs → torus → twist → squash) ·
04 drop: 26k particles, curl-noise flow → form 動態設計 → gust ·
05 8 rapid cuts, one per half beat (data viz, glitch, UI, line art, halftone, isometric, radial, ridgelines) ·
06 3×3 grid of live tiles → shuffle → zoom into the center ·
07 CLAUDE bursts from the dot, the dot lands as the period, "MOTION DESIGNER / 動態設計師", "AVAILABLE FOR NEW PROJECTS".
HUD with timecode and beat meter, adaptive motion blur, shake + flash on impacts, grain.
Synthesized soundtrack in F minor (Tone.js), drop at 7.5 s, −14 LUFS.
Build as a seekable renderFrame(f) HTML page, rendered with Playwright + ffmpeg.
```
