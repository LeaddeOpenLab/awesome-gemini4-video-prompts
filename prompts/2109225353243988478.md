## Prompt (English)

```text
Build a 25-second motion graphics video using JS, synthesizing audio on a consistent beat with Web Audio. Treat it as the finest piece in a portfolio: take creative liberty, pursuing bold art direction, striking compositions, and dynamic power. Clarify the pain point or spark curiosity within the first three seconds. Structure the narrative around audience comprehension so every motion and transition has causal logic. When the core value appears, combine animation and sound design to create an impactful Aha Moment. Synchronize key motions precisely to the beat, maintaining a steady and predictable rhythm while layering melody and instrumentation. Allocate duration thoughtfully to give viewers time to absorb essential information, avoiding pointless pauses and rushed cuts. Ensure typography is clean and legible, with refined layout, negative space, and details, ensuring every second carries weight and the final frame functions standalone as a polished design work.
```
