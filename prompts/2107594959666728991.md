## Prompt (English)

```text
Make a 30s 16:9 video (1920×1080, 30 fps) in HyperFrames (npx skills add heygen-com/hyperframes), with voiceover, matching the quality standards below.

## REQUEST
- Topic: Polymarket gives 84% chance to Flávio Bolsonaro in the 2026 runoff (10/25).
- Positive focus on Flávio across four points: 1. the market that predicted Trump in 2024 points to Flávio; 2. first-round polls favored Lula, but Flávio won; 3. Flávio got 5 million more votes than his father in 2022; 4. Lula's administration banned Polymarket in Brazil.
- CTA: Share so more people see the facts.
- Destination: X and LinkedIn.

## FACTS
- Polymarket, 10/06/2026: Flávio 84.2% vs Lula 15.5%, $174M volume.
- US 2024: Polymarket had Trump 58.1% vs Harris 41.9%.
- Round 1 2026 Polls vs TSE Result: Flávio 47.03% (56,104,503) vs Lula 45.16%.
- Ban: CMN Res 5,298/2026.

## RULES
- Betting odds are not election polls; label accordingly.
- Display 'AI generated voiceover' badge.
- Free-license Wikimedia photos only.

## SCRIPT
[dramatically] O mercado que acertou o Trump dá oitenta e quatro por cento pro Flávio. [serious] No primeiro turno, cinco de seis institutos davam Lula na frente. [dramatically] Deu Flávio. Cinquenta e seis milhões de votos. Cinco milhões a mais que o pai em vinte e dois. [serious] E quem proibiu o Polymarket no Brasil? O governo Lula. Não é pesquisa. É dinheiro de verdade. [dramatically] Compartilhe. Mais gente precisa ver os fatos.

## VOICE
- ElevenLabs eleven_v4, voice 'Randel - Deep Epic'. Align cuts to Whisper timestamps.

## SCENES
0. Cover (0-1s): Flávio photo, Polymarket 84% text.
1. Trump portrait, 58% badge.
2. Counter 0->84%.
3. Polls paper graphic.
4. TSE result.
5. Vote comparison bar charts.
6. Planalto Palace and ban decree.
7. Stock exchange background.
8. Street crowd and share CTA.

## VISUAL DIRECTION
- Palette: #100F0D bg, Newsreader and IBM Plex Mono typography, Ken Burns pan effects, GSAP timeline.

## AUDIO
- ElevenLabs music prompt, SFX on cuts, master -14 LUFS.

## DELIVERABLE
Save polymarket-flavio-30s-horizontal.mp4.
```
