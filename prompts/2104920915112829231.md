```text
Make me the most insane and blast of a shooter you can possibly build on ThreeJS + Web shaders bro!The most important things are shooting, dynamics, recoil, graphics, hit impact, gunplay. It's a beautifully designed arena where enemies swarm in hordes. Must have ADS aiming, weapon inertia/weight.3 weapon types: assault rifle / submachine gun, shotgun, and a heavy-hitting marksman rifle.Ability to attach (scopes, silencers, grips). This is a Call Of Duty Style AAA game in the browser. Do it right bro, I believe in you!This is your resume at Activision, do the maximum you can. More graphics, more engagement effects, more impact. You have only one shot at this. It should be a modern setting, modern combat, dirty trenches, drones, fog, drizzling rain. The style of the game is Bodycam, as close to realism as possible.Give yourself fully to this.
```
