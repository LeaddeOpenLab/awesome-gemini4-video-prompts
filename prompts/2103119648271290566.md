## Prompt (English)

```text
Whenever a new model comes out, everyone asks it to draw a "pelican riding a bicycle" to evaluate its spatial capabilities, mostly animating it using HTML and SVG. Since I'm genuinely tired of seeing the same thing over and over, I want you to create the most complex, detailed, and exquisite animated video of a "pelican riding a bicycle." You can use any technology, take your time, even if it takes a whole day.
```
