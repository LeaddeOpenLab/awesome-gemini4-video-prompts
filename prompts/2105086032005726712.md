## Prompt (English)

```text
Build me a 3D endless runner game in the browser, like Subway Surfers. A little robot runs down a neon city street at night. Left and right arrow keys switch between 3 lanes, up jumps, down slides. Collect coins, jump over barriers, slide under signs, and dodge trains. The speed goes up the longer you survive. Show the score and coins at the top, a start screen, and a game over screen with a restart button. Make it look like a real game from the App Store.

One HTML file. Do not ask me any questions. Just build it.
```
