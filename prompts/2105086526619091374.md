## Prompt (English)

```text
Little Star of the Moonlit Sea
A shooting star falls into the night sea, turning into a glowing little star; it sinks to the turquoise sea floor, lighting up sea anemones one by one as it passes. Finally, a sea turtle carries it back to the surface, and it flies back beside the moon, once again becoming a star in the sky.

The soundtrack is in an original Celtic lullaby style (D Dorian mode, modulating to D major at the end): harp arpeggios, bagpipe drone, string pads, and tin whistle lead melody, embellished with ocean waves, underwater bubbles, and chimes.

The visuals are divided into five scenes, with cross-dissolve transitions between them:

Moonlit sea surface, a shooting star falls into the ocean, title appears
The little star sinks into the turquoise sea water, kelp swaying, light beams and bubbles descending, opening its eyes underwater
Dark seabed, sea anemones light up one by one as the little star passes
A sea turtle carries it, swimming upwards toward the light
The sea turtle surfaces, the little star flies back beside the moon, ending subtitle: 'Every little light finds its way home'
```
